Privacy Policy — FPS Overlay

Last updated: 2026-09-06

FPS Overlay is a simple utility app that displays the device's current screen frame rate on top of its own window. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data collection

FPS Overlay does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. The app has no network permission and makes no network requests of any kind.

Data stored on device

The app saves your chosen font size, font, and color preferences locally on your device using Android's standard app-private storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — used only to draw the FPS counter on top of other apps and games. It is never used to read, capture, or interact with the content of other apps.
  • Foreground service — keeps the overlay running while you use other apps, with a persistent low-priority notification as required by Android.
  • Notifications — used solely to show that status notification; no other notifications are ever sent.

None of these permissions are used to collect, read, or transmit any data. The app still makes no network requests.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the developer via the app's Google Play listing.
